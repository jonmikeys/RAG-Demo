Obsidian vault demo

Notes already have structure: headings mark where one idea ends and the next begins. This page splits each note along its headings, labels every chunk with its note and section, and lets you compare that against blind fixed-size chunking. Your vault never leaves this browser tab.

1 Add a vault

Drop your vault folder, or choose it. Only .md notes are read; hidden folders such as .obsidian are skipped. Folder drop needs a desktop browser. Your browser may say "upload": the files stay local.
